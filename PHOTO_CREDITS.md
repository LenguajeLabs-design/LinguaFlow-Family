## Processing

The six activity photographs were cropped consistently to a 3:2 landscape frame, resized to 960 × 640 pixels, and exported as WebP at quality 82. Supporting and Parent Academy photographs were resized to a maximum dimension of 960 pixels and exported as optimized JPEGs at quality 72, preserving useful portrait or landscape framing. No faces, objects, or documentary details were altered.

## Selection principles

- Natural-looking home environments and shared family routines
- Warm, candid interaction rather than posed classroom imagery
- A mix of family structures and cultural representation
- No prominent logos, school identifiers, or readable personal information
- Enough visual space to remain legible within mobile activity cards

Before a public commercial launch, recheck the current Pexels license and review whether any additional model or property permissions are appropriate for the final marketing context.
